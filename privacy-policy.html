Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Introduction

MWX DigiLabs ("we", "our", "us") operates the Lotto Tracker PH mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

Information We Collect

Lotto Tracker PH does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. The app does not require user registration or login.

Data Usage

  • Lotto Results: Fetched from public sources (PCSO) and displayed within the app. No user-specific data is stored.
  • Ticket Scanner: Images captured for ticket scanning are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to any server.
  • Lucky Numbers: Generated locally on-device using statistical algorithms. No data is sent externally.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Displays advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Camera Permission

The app requests camera access solely for the ticket scanning feature. Photos are processed on-device and never uploaded.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
