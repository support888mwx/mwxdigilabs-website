Privacy Policy

Daily Bible & Rosary

Last updated: June 2025

Introduction

MWX Digital Labs ("we", "our", "us") operates the Daily Bible & Rosary mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Daily Bible & Rosary does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information to external servers. The app does not require user registration or login.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never uploaded or shared:

  • Prayer Journal Entries: Your personal prayer notes and reflections are saved on-device only.
  • Prayer Reminders: Notification preferences and schedules are stored locally.
  • Reading History: Your Bible reading progress and bookmarks are saved on-device.
  • App Preferences: Settings such as font size, theme, and notification preferences.

Bible & Prayer Content

Daily Bible verses, rosary prayers, devotions, and reflections are either bundled within the app or fetched from public, freely available sources. No personal data is exchanged during content delivery.

Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: May display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics (optional): May collect anonymous usage statistics to help us improve the app experience. No personally identifiable information is collected.

Notifications

The app may request permission to send local notifications for prayer reminders and daily verse alerts. These notifications are generated locally on your device and do not involve any external server communication.

Children's Privacy

Daily Bible & Rosary is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require any personal data to function.

Data Security

Since all personal data (journal entries, bookmarks, preferences) is stored locally on your device, it is protected by your device's own security features. We do not have access to this data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
